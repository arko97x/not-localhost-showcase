The premise

Why a café, and why incubation.

Incubation Café is a 10-week module inside the second-year MDes studio: a running bench where ideas get poured out early, tasted while they're rough, and sent back for another round. It sits between a brief and a finished thesis project — the part usually kept private.

This site is that bench made public. Fifteen designers, fifteen concurrent projects, each logged at whatever stage it happens to be in — grounds, brewing, steeping, or freshly poured. Nothing here is final, and that's the point: it's a record of work being made, not work that's done.

Reading the labels

What the tags on each card mean.

Every card carries two labels: a brew stage (how far along the project is) and a discipline (what kind of design problem it is). Here's what each one means.

Brew stage — how far along
Grounds

Just started. The problem is picked, but there's no direction yet — still figuring out what to even design.

Brewing

Concepting. Several directions are being sketched and tested; nothing is locked in.

Steeping

Refining. One direction has been chosen and is being pressure-tested and reworked.

Poured

Presentable. There's a working prototype or draft, ready to show and get real feedback on.

Discipline — what kind of project
Product

A physical or digital object someone would use or own.

Service

A process or system of interactions across people, touchpoints and time.

Research

A study into a behaviour, group or problem — understanding, not yet solving.

Interaction

How a person and a system communicate — gesture, sound, touch, interface.

Systems

A framework, toolkit or shared language used across a larger structure.
