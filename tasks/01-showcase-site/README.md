# Task 01: Explore the showcase site

This repository will become a site that documents and shows off the work of everyone in the class. What should it look like?

Make **at least one** exploration of what our collective documentation and showcase site could be.

Put each exploration in its own iteration folder inside your `task-01/` folder, such as `your-name/task-01/iteration-01/`. Include a few lines on the idea behind it.

See [Sharing your work](../../README.md#sharing-your-work) for how to add your files and push them.
